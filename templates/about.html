About NEC AlgoViz Team

Democratizing computer science through premium, interactive, real-time algorithm visualization.

Our Vision

We envision NEC AlgoViz becoming a reliable and continuously evolving learning platform that makes algorithm visualization easier and more engaging for students. Starting as an academic tool for our college, we aim to expand the platform with more algorithms, richer visualizations, and improved interactive learning experiences that can benefit a wider community of learners.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build NEC AlgoViz as a practical and accessible algorithm visualization platform that helps students understand complex algorithms through interactive, step-by-step visual learning. Developed with the goal of making it available through our college website, the platform aims to serve as a useful academic resource for students while continuously evolving through new algorithms, improved visualizations, and contributions inspired by the wider algorithm-visualization community.

Future Scope

Upcoming features and strategic objectives to scale NEC AlgoViz's capabilities.

Phase 1 — Graph Algorithms

Kruskal's & Dijkstra's Algorithms

Adding step-by-step visualizations for Kruskal's minimum spanning tree construction and Dijkstra's single-source shortest path traversal with dynamic distance matrix overlays.

Phase 2 — Search & Traversal

BFS & DFS Visualizations

Implementing visual exploration stacks and queues for Breadth-First Search (BFS) and Depth-First Search (DFS) node exploration.

Phase 3 — Tree Data Structures

Binary Search Tree & AVL Tree

Expanding interactive tree canvas to support self-balancing AVL rotations, height factors, and BST insert/delete operations.

Phase 4 — Advanced Platform Features

Sorting, Analytics, Accounts & Export

Developing a Sorting Visualizer comparison grid, Algorithm Performance Comparison metrics, User Accounts for saving matrices, and Export Visualization image/code generators.
