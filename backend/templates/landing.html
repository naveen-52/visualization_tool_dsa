About NEC AlgoViz

NEC AlgoViz is an interactive algorithm visualization platform designed to simplify complex Data Structures and Algorithms through real-time execution and step-by-step visual learning. Built for students, developers, and problem solvers, the platform transforms abstract logic—such as Graph Coloring, Minimum Spanning Trees, and Huffman Coding—into clear, engaging, and intuitive educational experiences.
