How It Works

Graph Coloring is a fundamental problem in graph theory that ensures adjacent vertices connected by an edge never share the same color. NEC AlgoViz transforms this abstract concept into an interactive visual experience, helping you observe how colors are dynamically assigned across graph networks in real time.

Through the Welsh-Powell Algorithm, learners explore a systematic strategy that produces conflict-free colorings by prioritizing high-degree vertices. Understand the algorithm's decision-making process across three guided learning phases:

  1. Vertex Prioritization: Observe how vertices are ordered by degree, giving precedence to highly connected nodes to optimize color distribution.
  2. Sequential Color Assignment: Watch the algorithm assign the first available color to non-adjacent vertices step by step without creating conflicts.
  3. Conflict Validation: Inspect the final visual graph to verify that all adjacent node conflicts are eliminated and the optimal chromatic solution is achieved.
